Research direction

Urban agriculture, beyond food production.

I am interested in urban agriculture as multifunctional urban infrastructure. My goal is to understand when and how urban growing spaces can deliver ecological benefits alongside reliable food access and stronger local communities — and where those goals may compete.

Questions I want to investigate

01. How do different urban agricultural systems influence food access, biodiversity, pollinator communities, and community resilience?
02. Which management practices best balance food production with pollinator habitat and other ecosystem services?
03. What synergies and trade-offs emerge between ecological outcomes, local economic activity, and equitable food access?

Research themes

I am especially interested in research that can connect ecological measurement with practical decisions about how cities plan, fund, and manage green spaces.
